# High-Level Summary

Space Heroes is a browser game about designing a science mission before anyone flies it. A player takes one seat on a small team, writes the science question, builds a spacecraft under six hard limits, and then flies a trajectory that was propagated in NASA's General Mission Analysis Tool. The point is to make mission design understandable: every choice spends budget, mass, power, data, communications, or time, and the debrief compares the result with a real NASA mission.

This answers the Design and Develop Science Missions challenge. Instead of a slide about an imaginary spacecraft, the player has to keep a concept inside a Concept Maturity Level gate, choose real classes of parts, survive three tests, and watch an orbit whose numbers come from GMAT R2026a. Eight chapters are playable, from a 4 kg low-Earth CubeSat to the Moon, Mars, a Sun-Earth L1 halo, a polar orbit, a high orbit, and geostationary orbit. A local party of four can share the same gauges.

The project matters because the hard part of a science mission is not the rocket picture. It is the trade. Space Heroes keeps that trade visible, in French or English, on a phone or a laptop, without asking the player to run GMAT.
